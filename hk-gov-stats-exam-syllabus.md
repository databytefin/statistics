## Part 2: Core Statistical Knowledge

### A. Descriptive Statistics & Data Exploration

- Variables: random, continuous, discrete, qualitative, quantitative
- Central tendency: mean (arithmetic; geometric/harmonic at definition level), median, mode
- Dispersion: variance (population / sample / unbiased), SD, MAD, range, IQR, CV
- Shape: skewness, kurtosis
- Percentiles & quartiles; outlier detection (IQR)
- Grouped vs ungrouped data; frequency tables; histograms
- Scales: Nominal → Ordinal → Interval → Ratio (and matching statistics)

### A′. EDA, Visualization & Data Quality

- EDA flow: question → types → summaries → plots → missing/outliers
- Chart choice: histogram, bar, boxplot, scatter, line (avoid misleading pie/3D)
- Missing values & outliers: method + domain judgment
- Sample vs population formulas; biased vs unbiased \(s^2\)

### B. Probability & Sampling

- Events; addition/multiplication; conditional probability; Bayes
- Joint & marginal; combinations & permutations
- Sampling: SRS, stratified, cluster, systematic; non-probability & bias
- CLT; LLN; sampling distributions; degrees of freedom
- Finite population correction (concept)

### C. Probability Distributions

- Discrete: Bernoulli, Binomial, Poisson, Hypergeometric, Geometric
- Continuous: Uniform, Normal, Standard Normal, t, Exponential, Chi-square
- PDF / PMF / CDF; Z-score; effect of μ, σ
- When mean & SD are inappropriate (e.g. multimodal)

### D. Statistical Inference

- Point vs interval estimation; unbiasedness; E[X]; SE vs SD; CI
- H₀/H₁; Type I/II; α, p-value; one- vs two-tailed
- t-test, Chi-square, ANOVA; non-parametric awareness
- Contingency tables & independence; power (concept)

### E. Correlation, Regression, Index Numbers & Time Series

- Pearson vs Spearman; covariance; correlation ≠ causation
- Simple & multiple regression; R², Adj. R²; F-test
- Residual checks (linearity, equal variance, independence, normality)
- Index numbers: Laspeyres, Paasche, Fisher
- Time series basics: trend, seasonality, moving averages

**Deprioritized (skip detail):** Gamma; radar/bubble/Pareto/donut; dedup / cleaning-vs-wrangling jargon; harmonic-mean formulas

## Part 3: Hong Kong Official Statistics

### F. Population & Demographics

- Usually resident vs mobile; age structure; dependency ratios
- Census vs by-census

### G. Labour Market

- Labour force, participation, unemployment, underemployment
- Industry / occupation classifications

### H. National Accounts

- GDP (production / expenditure / income); nominal vs real; per capita
- GNI / net external factor income

### I. Prices & Index Interpretation

- CPI basket & weights; inflation reading
- Laspeyres / Paasche / Fisher; base change; seasonal adjustment vs raw
- Real wages / purchasing power

### J. Other Official Series

- Retail sales, trade, housing-related indicators (source & use)

### Interpretation pitfalls

- Unemployment ↑ ≠ always weaker economy
- CPI ≠ personal “felt” inflation
- Nominal GDP ↑ may be prices only — check real GDP / deflator
- Always read definition, coverage, revision, seasonal adjustment

### Read charts & sampling error

- SE / CI language; SA vs original series; careful comparison across base changes
